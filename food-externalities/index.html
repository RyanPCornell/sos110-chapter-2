How to read this graph: The market equilibrium (where Supply meets Demand) shows the private price. When negative externalities exist — such as carbon emissions and environmental damage from food production — the true Social Marginal Cost is higher than the market price. Switch to Social View to see the full externality picture, or Policy to explore how a Pigovian tax can correct this market failure.
